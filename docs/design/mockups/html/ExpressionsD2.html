How an expression is built

An expression is a handful of numbers, not a new drawing.

1. Eye shape: open, arc, squeeze, star, X. This carries most of the emotion and survives at 40 px.
2. Lid openness and pupil size. Cheap to animate: two numbers.
3. Brows. Readable on the picker and Death screens, mostly lost in game.
4. Mouth: smile, open, O, wave, grin, flat, chew. Anchored on mouthY.
5. Extras: sweat, spark, zzz. Drawn outside the face so the head silhouette stays clean.

Rules

• One expression at a time. Priority high to low: dead, hurt, win, scared, happy, determined, alert, chew, sleepy, idle.
• Reactions are short (400-800 ms) and return to idle. Only dead, win, scared and sleepy hold.
• Blend by tween (about 120 ms) on the numbers, never by swapping images, so it never pops.
• Expressions are triggered by game events only. No aim and no player control.
• Reduced motion: keep the static face, drop blink, wander, tongue flick and chew pulse.
• Brows and extras are bonus. If a state is unreadable at 40 px without them, change the eye or mouth instead.
