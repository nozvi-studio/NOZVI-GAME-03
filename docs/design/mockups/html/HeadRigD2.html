Anchors

One 48 x 48 box. Every skin hangs its face on the same points.

Layer order

1ears + inner ear
2head shape (gradient)
3marks x2 (stripes, diamonds, blaze)
4muzzle + cheeks
5tongue (root sits on the mouth anchor)
6nose / nostrils
7mouth: idle, eat, dead (all hang on mouthY)
8eyes: open + blink + wander, or X for dead

Rules

• No leaf, stem or fruit shape on any head. The mango is food, so a head must never read as one.
• Tongue root touches the mouth line. Nostrils sit above the smile. Eyes and mouth stay on the anchors.
• Eyes must stay readable at 40 px. Check on light, ice and dark grounds; add an outline if contrast is under 3:1.
• Every skin ships idle, eat and dead. Glow is off in pickers and shop cards.
• Heads stay front facing. Body, trail and rotation belong to the game, not to the head.

Add a skin

1. Copy one record in PlayerHead SKINS. Change colours first.
2. Keep eyes, nose and mouth on the shared anchors. Move only the head shape, ears and marks.
3. Fill idle, eat and dead. Leave a slot empty rather than inventing a new layer.
4. Open this board and check 40 px on all three grounds.
5. Rare and above: swap the vector layers for a raster atlas that uses the same anchors and states.
