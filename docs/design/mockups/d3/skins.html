Mango Snake · Characters screen directions (P4a)

Three directions for the Characters (skins) screen in the D3 "Juicy" language, 360×640. Replaces the flat SkinsD3 / SkinBuyD2 boards. Snake and Capy heads are the game rig; Ginger, Pup and Tuxedo are placeholder drawings on the same anchors. Mock data: 120 coins, Mango equipped, Ginger owned. Mockups only.

A · Stage + shelf

The character stands on a podium and moves (idle bob, blink, Rare sparkles). One row of round tokens under it: tap = preview on the stage. One big action button whose label is the state (Equipped / EQUIP / BUY 300 / Need 680 more).
